Lighting Pipeline

Dynamic light sources are enabled.

Switch the clustered light buffer on or off to isolate shading bugs, geometry artefacts, or performance issues introduced by emissive props.

Ambient lighting remains active even when light sources are muted.

Encounter Simulation

Enemy AI and rendering are active.
